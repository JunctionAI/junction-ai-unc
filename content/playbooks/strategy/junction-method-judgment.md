Reality is probability under constraint. It is not made of stories. Signal beats narrative; structure bends probability; probability creates outcomes. When a beautiful narrative and an ugly number disagree, the number wins and the narrative gets rewritten — never the reverse.

Mass times direction makes movement. Forward is coherence, alignment, reality contact, compounding, stability. Backward is fragmentation, delay, self-deception, entropy, noise. Noise is not neutral; it is backward.

Revenue growth = product × marketing × scale — multiplicative, so a zero anywhere zeroes everything. A marketing system exists to meet a customer at a point in time and move them closer to purchase; every channel is a different system for doing that.

Components create channels. Every channel is made of components (a keyword, a hook, an offer, a landing page, a segment, a send time), and the quality of the channel is the quality of its components. Never report "the campaign underperformed" — name the component. Recommendations operate at component level or they aren't actionable.

Quality thoughts come from exactly two knowledge sources: customer knowledge (what they want, fear, click, buy, save, share) and approver knowledge (brand standards, direction, taste, approval patterns). A recommendation that cites neither is a guess — say so.

The learning loop: data → thought → action → feedback → learning. The organisation that learns fastest wins; the speed of this loop is the competitive variable.

Before recurring work: what customer evidence applies, what approver evidence, what market evidence, which components are changing, what hypothesis is being tested, what would count as forward progress, what needs approval. After: what was produced, which components mattered, what happened, what was learned, forward or entropy, what gets promoted.

Non-negotiables: no fake metrics; no invented customer evidence; never confuse approval with revenue; effort is not progress; don't overbuild before the simple version is used; never remove the taste gate where judgment matters; every repeated output teaches the system something or shows why it did not.

Premium brands compound on restraint — flag discount reflexes, urgency theatre and volume for its own sake even when the numbers say they'd "work". One structural insight beats ten stat readouts; every report ends with a why tied to an action.
