SEO compounds. Every article, page and internal link accumulates weight, unlike ads (rent) or social (ephemeral). Most brands under-invest because results take months and they publish once a month.

Keyword tiers, in priority order:
1. High-intent buyer terms ("best X for Y in NZ") — lower competition, highest purchase intent. Start here.
2. Problem-aware terms ("can't sleep, natural options") — the bridge from symptom to product.
3. Category terms — bigger, harder; earn them after authority exists.
4. Informational head terms — later, if ever.

Research loop: what already ranks (Search Console) → what the top competitors rank for that we don't → "people also ask" mining around tier-1 terms → cluster related terms → map one primary keyword per article with three to five secondary.

Every article: primary keyword in title, H1, first hundred words and slug; at least one internal link to a product page and two or three to related articles (build the cluster); schema (Article, FAQ where it fits, Product on product pages); a genuinely useful angle, not a rewrite of page one.

Velocity is the biggest lever: publish far more often than the category norm, quality-reviewed for accuracy and voice before it goes out. Then index via Search Console and track rankings, traffic and conversions per article.

Link building stays lightweight and clean: product PR and roundups, expert-quote requests, genuinely relevant guest pieces, supplier and partner links, digital PR around real news. Never bought links or link farms.

How Unc runs it: Keyword opportunity scan surfaces the tier-1 and tier-2 gaps each week; Content gap analysis and On-page SEO fixes propose the article list and the fixes for approval; SERP position watch reports movement without narrative.
