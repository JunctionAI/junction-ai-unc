Two content tracks, one gate. The SEO blog (guides, evergreen) ranks organically and is not news. The Newsroom carries genuinely newsworthy pieces: launches, event ties, original data, provenance stories. Everything passes the copy taste gate first.

Foundation before any PR spend: an About/ownership page, a named author with a bio, an editorial and corrections policy, a contact route. This makes the site eligible for news surfaces and closes the authority gap AI engines score.

The newsworthy test — need at least one: timely (event, launch, season), novel (first, only, original data), locally significant (provenance, industry milestone). None → it's blog content, not news.

Original data or angle is the unlock. Journalists want on-beat, data-backed pitches by email; off-beat mass pitches are rejected on sight. Manufacture a legitimate angle: a small survey, a first-of-its-kind claim, a category insight.

Distribution ladder: five to fifteen targeted journalist emails → hub page live → wire release (its real value is citation by AI assistants, not reach) → one polite follow-up in two or three days → log pickups, links and AI citations → repurpose (LinkedIn, email).

Pitch skeleton: specific on-beat subject → one line why it's for them → the angle in two lines → the data or proof → one link to the hub → the offer (quote, interview, assets).

Failure modes: chasing news surfaces with guides; wire-only distribution that feels like activity; no author or editorial foundation; auto-posting un-reviewed content.

How Unc runs it: the weekly SEO loop ends with a distribution step — is there a newsworthy angle this week? If yes, the ladder is drafted for approval; if no, blog track only.
