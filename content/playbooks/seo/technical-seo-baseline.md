Deploy during onboarding, re-check monthly. Content velocity on a broken site is wasted.

The checklist:
- Site speed: Core Web Vitals passing on mobile.
- Fully responsive; HTTPS everywhere.
- XML sitemap auto-generated and submitted to Search Console; robots.txt not blocking important pages.
- Canonical tags set correctly (especially on collections, variants and filtered pages).
- Schema: Product on product pages, Article on posts, FAQ where questions are answered; category-specific schema where it exists.
- Internal linking: every product page links to related content and vice versa.
- No broken internal links; 404s monitored.
- Unique, keyword-aware titles and meta descriptions on every page.
- Indexation rate: published pages indexed, not just crawled.

Agent-readiness (the newer layer): product info, price, variants, delivery, returns and the call to action in stable, predictable places with consistent selectors — pages are now read by agents that transact, not only crawlers that index. Feed, product page and structured data must agree exactly.

How Unc runs it: On-page SEO fixes lists the failing checks with the specific page and the proposed fix; the founder approves fixes in a batch; nothing changes on the site without that okay.
