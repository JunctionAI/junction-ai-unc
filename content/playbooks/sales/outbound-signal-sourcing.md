The differentiator in outbound is the signal layer, not the sending layer. Propensity beats firmographics.

Architecture: signal → filter → enrich → (send) → manage.
1. Signal sourcing: a small set of niche influencers and business pages whose engagers are hand-raisers. Net-new posts are scanned on a schedule and every engager (likes, comments) is captured. Engagement is the signal.
2. ICP-fit filter before enrichment: research the person and company (size, stack, geography) and only fits proceed. Never pay to enrich a non-fit.
3. Waterfall enrichment, cheapest source first, stop on hit; validate every email and only ever send to validated addresses. Store every result with its source and confidence.
4. Sending, if it happens at all: never from the core business domain. Four domain classes — cold, marketing, transactional, the business domain — and the business domain never sends volume. Consent-based regimes (NZ/AU) need a localised consent model before any volume; a shortlist plus personally-sent notes is the trust-led alternative.
5. Inbox management: a positive reply triggers a goal-prompted thread handler that drives to the booking link, answers questions, and checks the calendar for actual conversions; cold threads are revived on long cycles.

For a trust-led business, adopt steps one to three as the research upgrade and skip volume sending: the output is a ranked shortlist with a reason per name, and the founder sends the notes.

How Unc runs it: Lead research and scoring produces the shortlist with evidence; Supervised outbound drafts writes the note per person for approval; nothing is sent without the founder's okay, ever.
