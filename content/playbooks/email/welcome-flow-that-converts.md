The welcome series does most of the heavy lifting for turning a new subscriber into a first purchase, so it is the first flow to build and the first to tune.

Shape: five to seven emails over about fourteen days. One job per email, one primary call to action per email.

1. Immediately — welcome, the brand story in a paragraph, and deliver whatever was promised at sign-up (the reciprocity gift). Set expectations for what comes next.
2. Day 1 — the founder's story or the mission. Why this exists, in the founder's own words.
3. Day 3 — best-seller showcase or the core education piece (how it works, what makes it different).
4. Day 5 — social proof: real customer stories, quoted verbatim, first names only.
5. Day 7 — product education: how to use it, how to get the most from it.
6. Day 10 — the incentive reminder, framed as a nudge not a shout.
7. Day 14 — final nudge plus the value proposition restated.

Rules that hold:
- Subject line does half the work. Two angles per test (curiosity vs direct), under ten words, mobile-legible. Preview text is a second subject line — it extends the hook, never repeats it.
- Inverted pyramid body: the most important thing first, benefit before feature, second person, sounds like a friend. A skimmer reading only the top must still get it.
- Anyone who buys exits the flow (profile filter re-checked before every send). Never welcome someone who has already purchased.
- Grade on conversion and revenue per email, not opens.

How Unc runs it: the Welcome flow tuning routine reads the flow's step-by-step conversion, proposes one change at a time (subject line, send delay, the proof email's quote), and sends the proposal to the founder for approval before anything changes in Klaviyo.
