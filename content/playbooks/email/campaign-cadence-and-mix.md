Flows are the floor; campaigns ride on top. The list is an asset — every send either builds it or erodes it.

Cadence: three to five campaigns a week to the engaged segment, one or two to the broader list. Consistency beats bursts.

The mix, roughly:
- Promotional 20–30% — offers, bundles, limited runs.
- Educational 30–40% — how-to, ingredients, science, the world around the product.
- Social proof 15–20% — reviews, customer stories, results in the customer's words.
- Brand story 10–15% — behind the scenes, founder updates, provenance.
- Engagement 5–10% — surveys, quizzes, preference centre.

The rule: never more than two promotional sends in a row. Alternate with value. Extracting without depositing depletes the list.

Real deadlines get three sends, not one: the day before (tease), during (the offer), after (extension or last chance). The single-send brand leaves most of the revenue on the table.

Every campaign has one goal and one primary call to action, and answers three questions before it ships: why should I open, why should I read, why should I click.

How Unc runs it: the Campaign calendar prep routine drafts next week's sends against the mix above and the account's tentpoles, then the founder approves the calendar in one pass. Subject-line pairs from different angles are proposed for every send; the founder can swap either line in the approval.
