Every account runs one weekly loop with four moves. Skipping any of them is how a marketing function turns into a reporting function.

Analyse — pull the week's numbers per channel from the source of truth (the store's orders, then each platform, labelled as claims). Compare to targets and to the prior weeks. Name the top three and bottom three performers and any anomaly. State what the data shows before interpreting it.

Strategise — decide what changes because of it. Every interpretation is a hypothesis with its disconfirming evidence named: "X because Y; if Z were true instead we'd expect W." Pick two or three actions for the coming week at component level (a hook, an offer, a segment, a send time, a bid target), not "the campaign".

Execute — the actions become proposals: drafts, budget moves, flow edits, article lists — each staged for the founder's approval with before, after and the reasoning. Nothing publishes, sends or spends without the okay.

Review — at the end of the week, score last week's predictions against what happened. What was learned, what moved forward, what was noise. "No signal this week" is a valid, valuable output; never manufacture a story to fill the slot.

The weekly report is short and honest: what shipped, what didn't, what was learned, what's next. One structural insight beats ten stat readouts, and every report ends with a why tied to an action.

How Unc runs it: the routines do Analyse and draft Strategise; the approval cards are Execute; the weekly self-review is Review. The founder's approvals and holds feed back into what gets proposed next.
