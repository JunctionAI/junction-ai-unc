Meta is a machine-learning system whose targeting input is the creative itself. The job is not audience engineering; it is finding creative that resonates and turning winners into diverse volume.

Structure at modest spend:
- One consolidated prospecting campaign, broad targeting (age, country, gender only), Advantage+ where conversion signals and catalogue are clean. Split by product line only if the lines have genuinely different economics.
- A value-optimised duplicate of prospecting so the high-order-value customer is reachable — volume optimisation never finds them. For premium brands this lane is mandatory.
- Retargeting under about five percent of spend; skip it entirely at small budgets.
- No separate testing campaign below serious monthly spend: launch new ads straight into the scaling campaign and let the auction rank them. A dedicated testing lane earns its place only when learning contamination costs real money.

Economics live in bids, not budget fiddling: set the target (bid cap or target ROAS) and let the platform spend what it can while hitting it. When budget does move: plus twenty percent steps every few days, never doublings (learning-phase resets).

The platform rewards creative quality and volume, broad targeting, consistent spend and full-funnel structure. It punishes low creative volume, over-segmented audiences, start-stop budgets and attribution worship.

Exclusions always: recent purchasers out of acquisition. Refresh lookalike seeds monthly if lookalikes are used at all.

How Unc runs it: the Daily paid decisioning routine reads spend allocation and fatigue signals, proposes one structural or budget change at a time as an approval card with before/after, and the Budget pacing guard holds daily spend inside the founder's monthly budget as a hard rail.
