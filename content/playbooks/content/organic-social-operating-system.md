Organic social is a trust channel, not a revenue channel. It validates the brand when a prospect checks before buying, builds an audience that compounds, and feeds paid — every organic post is a free ad test.

Start with one or two platforms and do them well. For most DTC brands: Instagram first, TikTok second, then Facebook for older demographics, LinkedIn only for founder or B2B plays, Pinterest for visual categories.

Pillars (adapt per brand): education ~30%, product ~20%, social proof ~20%, behind the scenes ~15%, lifestyle and culture ~15%. No more than a fifth of posts are product posts — nobody follows a brand for product shots. Lead with value.

Formats by reach: short-form video > carousels > stories > static. Stories are the daily touchpoint; reels and carousels are the reach and education engines.

Cadence: consistency beats volume. A steady three posts a week every week beats seven for a fortnight then silence. Batch-plan and schedule weekly.

Community: reply to comments within hours in business time, DMs within a day; engage with tagged posts and UGC; repost the best UGC with permission. UGC is the strongest content type for both organic and paid.

The organic-to-paid pipeline: track engagement (saves, shares, watch time); the top slice of organic by engagement becomes ad creative. Gate promotion on engagement rate, not views — hostile virality amplified with money buys hostility.

Metrics worth watching: engagement rate and save rate over follower count; reach as a share of followers; pieces of UGC collected; creatives graduated to paid.

How Unc runs it: the Founder content engine plans the week from the pillars, drafts captions and hooks for approval, and Content performance learning reports which pillar and format resonated — as data, before any story about why.
