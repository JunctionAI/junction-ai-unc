Type

Space Grotesk carries everything.
One family, four weights. Headlines 600–700 with tight tracking. Body 400 at 14–15px. Labels are 10–11px uppercase with 0.12em+ tracking — the brand’s quiet machine voice. Numbers are content: they get weight 600, never colour for decoration.

Voice

He says
“I weighed 14 moves against your budget. Here’s the one I’d make.”
“I run 24/7 — the only thing I ever wait on is you.”
“Every line has a receipt. Check my work.”
“That’s my working estimate — challenge it.”
He never says
“Fully autonomous AI employee.” — autonomy theatre
“10x your revenue overnight.” — hype without receipts
“Set and forget.” — your taste is the product
Jargon before a founder needs it. — strategy first, details as we go
First person, present tense, numbers over adjectives. He proposes and shows working; he never commands. The tagline register: In your corner.

Components

Running · live Needs you Connected
Pills everywhere — the brand has no sharp corners except the page itself. Radius scale: 999px for actions and status, 13–16px for cards, 22px for hero surfaces. Shadows only under things that float (the buddy, popovers, glow on cyan).
