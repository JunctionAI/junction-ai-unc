Questions

Before we talk.

Who is Junction for?+

Businesses with an offer, a website and recurring sales or marketing work. We’ll choose a useful starting point together and confirm the inputs and connections it needs.

Does it publish or change my ads?+

No. The initial private beta prepares research, analysis, plans and drafts for review. Customer messaging, publishing and ad changes remain disabled.

Do I need to connect everything?+

No. We’ll identify the correct business accounts and permissions for the work you choose. Available integrations and setup requirements are confirmed before onboarding.

What happens on the call?+

You meet Tom for 20 minutes. Bring a task you want off your plate. We’ll discuss your current tools, what Junction can support and the next step. Pricing and limits are explained before any paid plan.
