Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it. Written to be read, not to be survived.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Who we are

This site and our services are run by Junction Central Limited, trading as Junction AI — a New Zealand company (company number 7879076, NZBN 9429047921313), registered at 17 Norfolk Street, Ponsonby, Auckland, New Zealand.

We build and run AI marketing agents for businesses. That means we handle two very different things: information about you, and information that belongs to our clients. Those are treated differently, and this policy keeps them separate.

The three kinds of information we handle

Most privacy policies blur these together. Ours doesn’t, because the rules genuinely differ.

1. People who visit this website or talk to us. If you read this site, join our email list or book a call, we decide what happens to that information. This policy governs it.

2. People who work at our client companies. Names, work email addresses, and the messages we exchange while doing the work. This policy governs it.

3. Our clients’ own customers. When we run marketing for a business, we handle their customer data — orders, email subscribers, ad audiences. We hold that information for our client, not for ourselves. The client decides what happens to it. If you are a customer of one of our clients, their privacy policy is the one that applies to you, and their team is who to contact.

When you visit this website

Analytics and ad measurement

If you choose “Allow analytics”, we load Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. They help us count visits, understand which pages people read, measure which ads lead people to the site, and record when someone clicks through to book a call. We do not treat a click as a booked call or a sale.

These tools may set cookies and send page, browser, device, IP-address and advertising-attribution information to Google and Meta. We use that information to measure and improve our own marketing. We do not sell it. Choose “Essential only” to leave both tools off; the site works either way. You can change the choice later with the “Cookie choices” button.

If you join our email list

We ask for your email address, and optionally your website. We use them to send you what you asked for and a short series of follow-up emails about how we work. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing is instant and permanent. We do not sell, rent or share your email address with anyone.

If you book a call

Bookings run through Calendly, which collects your name, email and whatever you type into the booking form. We use it to hold the meeting and to follow up.

Server logs

Our host, Vercel, keeps standard access logs including IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running. These are short-lived.

When you are a client

To do the work, we need access to your marketing platforms — things like Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta, Google Ads, Pinterest, Google Search Console and Google Analytics. We connect using the access you grant us, and you can revoke it at any time.

We copy data from those platforms into a database so our agents can read accurate numbers instead of guessing. That database is separated by client.

Four things we do not do with client data

  • We do not pool it. One client’s data is never mixed with another’s, and never used to inform another client’s work.
  • We do not sell it, to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use it to train AI models. More on this below.
  • We do not publish your results without asking. If we want to write about work we did for you, we ask first and you approve what gets said.

AI models, and whether your data trains them

Our agents run on AI models from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI. Your information is sent to those models so they can do the work — read your numbers, draft your emails, analyse your ads.

We use these providers under commercial terms where business data is not used to train their models. We do not train any model on your data ourselves. We do not feed one client’s data into work for another.

This is the question we get asked most, so we’d rather answer it in writing than in a sales call.

Information going overseas

We are a New Zealand company, but the companies above are mostly in the United States, and some operate in Ireland and Australia. That means your information is stored and processed outside New Zealand.

Before using a provider we check that it offers comparable protections to those required under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, and we rely on the contractual terms each provider offers. If you are in Australia, this is our disclosure under Australian Privacy Principle 8.

How long we keep things

  • Email subscribers: until you unsubscribe, then we keep a record of the unsubscribe so we don’t email you again by mistake.
  • Enquiries and call notes: up to two years after we last spoke, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client data: for as long as we work together. When we stop, we delete or return it at your direction, other than what we must keep for tax and legal records.
  • Analytics and ad measurement: under the retention settings in our Google and Meta accounts.

Keeping it safe

Access is limited to the people who need it. Connections to your platforms use the platforms’ own secure methods, and credentials are stored separately from the data. Client data is separated by client in our database. No system is perfectly secure, and we won’t pretend otherwise — but if something goes wrong that puts you at risk of serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • give you a copy of it;
  • correct it if it’s wrong;
  • delete it, where we’re not required to keep it;
  • stop emailing you.

Email tom@getjunction.ai and we’ll respond within 20 working days, which is the New Zealand legal limit. There’s no charge.

If you are a customer of one of our clients, we probably hold your information on their behalf — so contact them first. Ask us and we’ll point you to the right place.

Cookies

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel stay off until you choose “Allow analytics”. “Essential only” leaves them off. We store your choice in your browser so we do not ask on every page, and the “Cookie choices” button lets you review it later.

Children

We sell to businesses. This site isn’t aimed at children and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant and we have your email address, we’ll tell you directly.

Contact us, or complain

Our privacy officer is Tom Hall-Taylor. Email tom@getjunction.ai, or write to Junction Central Limited, 17 Norfolk Street, Ponsonby, Auckland, New Zealand.

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled it, you can complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner. In Australia, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
